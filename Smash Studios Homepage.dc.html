Eight kinds of room, one building

    Recording studios

    Neve and API signal chain, four live rooms, iso booths, and a vintage outboard collection that stays in the building. Engineer optional.

    Rehearsal studios

The Stage Suite

1,690 square feet with a PA and a load-in

Large-format bands rehearse their full production here. Theatre companies block scenes at true stage width. Labels sit 75 people down for a showcase, and the video team streams it the same night.

  • Dimensions 65 × 26 ft, 16 ft ceilings
  • Capacity 75 seated, 120 standing
  • Included Stage lighting, PA, monitors, backline
  • Add-ons Multi-cam capture, live stream, house engineer
